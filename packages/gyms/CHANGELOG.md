# gyms: changelog

## Gym ladders
- **Kanto, exactly as in Red/Blue:** the 8 gyms, the Elite Four and the Champion, with the leaders' real teams and levels. Gym battles are 3 vs 3: a leader with more than three Pokémon keeps their ace and the next two strongest.
- **Every other region:** the first 3-4 gyms of Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola (grand trials), Galar and Paldea, 31 in all, with rosters from the original games.
- **Gym Moveset:** Modernized by default (more balanced), or Original, each leader's moves from their own game.
- **Smart Gym Leaders:** leaders use buffs, status and the right attack at the right time. Turn it off for random moves (much easier).
- **Elite Four: Classic Gauntlet:** the Elite Four and Champion back to back with the same three, healed between fights. Off: one at a time, like gyms.
- **Auto-battle**, set per battle or as the default, and an optional battle log while you review.
- **One legendary per gym team.**

## Pacing
- A gym is offered after a number of cards reviewed **in its region**. Each region keeps its own count, which pauses while you travel elsewhere, so arriving in a new region never unlocks its first gym on the spot.
- After a loss, a rematch opens after a set number of cards. "Not now" waits its own, shorter count.
- The HUD shows the count ("50 / 1200 until Rustboro Gym (Roxanne)"). Once every condition is met it says "Roxanne is ready to battle - next card".
- All three counts follow your daily card goal; see the recommendations in Settings.
- **Level caps:** in each region your Pokémon fight at no higher than the next leader's ace + 5. They keep their real level and EXP; earning badges raises the cap, and beating a region's Champion removes it.

## Badges
- Each region has its own badges. The badge case shows the region you're in, with art for all 44 badges (Kanto's 8 plus Elite Four and Champion emblems, and the other regions' intro gyms).

## Expeditions
- Three floors of defenders, then a legendary. Beat it to catch it.
- Damage, status and PP carry over between floors. Between floors you camp and can use Potions, Revives, Full Heals and Elixirs on anyone in the team; the legendary has to be beaten in one go.
- Cash and EXP are paid when the run ends.

## Works with
- **showdown:** battles follow the real games' rules. Without it, a built-in engine is used.
- **wild:** travel to the other regions, with their gyms, level caps in wild battles, and the Expeditions menu. Without it, gyms are Kanto only.
