# gyms

Gym ladders for all nine regions, with badges, level caps and legendary expeditions.

- `gym/` is the whole feature: the ladders (`gym_data.py`, `regions_data.py`), battles, the team picker, the badge case, expeditions (`expedition*.py`) and pacing per region.
- `user_files/sprites/gym_badges/` holds the badge art.

Works on its own, as Kanto only with the built-in battle engine.
- **With wild:** travel to the other regions, use their gyms and level caps, and open expeditions.
- **With showdown:** battles follow the real games' rules.

Settings: the **Gyms** group, plus the recommended values under your daily card goal.
