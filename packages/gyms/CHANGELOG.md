# gyms: changelog
## 2.6 (October 2026)
- **Plan ahead:** hover the HUD's gym counter while you review to see who's next, their team and the types to bring. The team picker shows the same three lines (the long notes are gone).
- **Move cards:** the battle window's move buttons show the move's type colour, category, power and accuracy, and whether it's super effective, not very effective or has no effect on the foe (its Tera type once it Terastallizes).
- **Level locks** replace the level caps (see Pacing). Moves a Pokémon couldn't know at the level it fights at are sealed: they show greyed as "Sealed · Lv X", and it uses its Scaled Moves (wild package) or moves it could know.
- **Double HP** in gym, rematch, expedition and legendary battles, so within a few levels a single hit rarely knocks a Pokémon out.
- Wild legendaries, totems and Titans are tougher (the Elite Four aren't).
- **Expeditions:** pick three, like gyms.
- **Classic Elite Four:** a loss restarts the run from the first member.
- **Mew:** meet your daily card goal 7 Anki days in a row, with Kanto's first three gyms beaten (in any region you're reviewing in), and Mew joins your team. Another 7-day streak earns another. Mew no longer appears in the wild.
- **Evolution items in rematch loot:** all 16 of Kanto's from Kanto's leaders, and every other region's from a leader of a fitting type there.
- **Legendaries can be shiny**, and you see it in the offer and the battle.
- The battle summary reads "Your Team vs Wild Mewtwo" with the legendary's own name.
- The HUD counter says "until you can retry Misty" after a loss and names the Elite Four or the Champion Cup, and sits a little higher above the HP boxes.
- **Phone reviews count:** after a sync, reviews from your other devices count toward gyms, rematches and expeditions without a restart (EXP and training points come from desktop reviews).


## Gym ladders
- **Kanto, exactly as in Red/Blue:** the 8 gyms, the Elite Four and the Champion, with the leaders' real teams and levels. Gym battles are 3 vs 3: a leader with more than three Pokémon keeps their ace and the next two strongest.
- **Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova and Kalos, full ladders like Kanto:** 8 gyms, the Elite Four and the Champion each, with the real teams and levels from Crystal, Ruby/Sapphire, Diamond/Pearl, Black/White and X/Y. Alder (Unova) fights at levels that follow the Elite Four rather than his post-game 75-77; Diantha's Gardevoir doesn't Mega Evolve.
- **Alola, the full Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon ladder:** 8 totem trials in the gym slots, each island's kahuna (Hala, Olivia, Nanu, Hapu) right after its last totem, then the Elite Four (Molayne, Olivia, Acerola, Kahili) and Hau. See Totem trials below.
- **Galar, the Sword/Shield ladder:** 8 gyms, then the Champion Cup (Marnie, Nessa, Bea or Allister, Raihan) and Leon. On your first visit you choose Sword or Shield: Bea or Allister, Gordie or Melony. The other version's leader waits in Gym Rematches once you've cleared that slot. See Galar below.
- **Paldea, open world:** the 8 gyms in any order, then the Elite Four, Geeta and Nemona, plus 5 optional Titans. See Paldea below.
- **Gym Moveset:** Modernized by default (more balanced), or Original, each leader's moves from their own game.
- **Smart Gym Leaders:** leaders use buffs, status and the right attack at the right time. Turn it off for random moves (much easier).
- **Elite Four: Classic Gauntlet:** the Elite Four and Champion back to back with the same three, healed between fights. Off: one at a time, like gyms.
- **Auto-battle**, set per battle or as the default, and an optional battle log while you review.
- **One legendary per gym team.**

## Pacing
- A gym is offered after a number of cards reviewed **in its region**. Each region keeps its own count, which pauses while you travel elsewhere, so arriving in a new region never unlocks its first gym on the spot.
- After a loss, a rematch opens after a set number of cards. "Not now" waits its own, shorter count.
- The HUD shows the count ("50 / 1200 until Rustboro Gym (Roxanne)"). Once every condition is met it says "Roxanne is ready to battle - next card".
- All three counts follow your daily card goal; see the recommendations in Settings.
- **Level locks:** against a leader, the Elite Four, a Champion, a rematch, an expedition or a legendary, your Pokémon fight at no higher than the opponent's strongest Pokémon (the old "next leader's ace + 5" cap is gone). They keep their real level and EXP, and lower-level Pokémon fight at their own level.

## Badges
- Each region has its own badges. The badge case shows the region you're in, with art for every one: the official badges where the games have them (Kanto to Kalos, Galar), drawn emblems for the rest (Alola, Paldea, Elite Fours and Champions).

## Expeditions
- Three floors of defenders, then a legendary. Beat it to catch it.
- Damage, status and PP carry over between floors. Between floors you camp and can use Potions, Revives, Full Heals and Elixirs on anyone in the team; the legendary has to be beaten in one go.
- Cash and EXP are paid when the run ends.

Legendaries follow the original games: the trio members that stay put and the box legendaries are expeditions; the ones that roam are rare wild encounters (wild package).

**Difficulty:** tuned on Pokémon Showdown's rules for three Pokémon with a type advantage, at your level cap when the expedition opens (after the Champion: the Champion's ace + 3), starting each fight at full health (you can heal at camp between floors):
- each legendary is won about 3 times in 4 (levels capped at 20 over that level: the Regis, Uxie, Azelf, Tapu Koko and Heatran are still a bit easier for a matched team);
- the floors get harder as you go but each is won at least 9 times in 10. The defenders that countered the types you'd bring for the legendary were swapped (Froslass at Snowpoint Temple, Pyroar at Lysandre Labs, Aggron at Zygarde's Hollow, Bronzong at Spear Pillar) or levelled down.
- A legendary is caught at the level you fought it; level locks still apply to it in battle.
- Galar's and Paldea's legendaries use the same level rule but haven't been through the simulations yet.

Expeditions can only be started in their own region (Game > Expeditions, from the wild package).

## Galar
- **Sword or Shield:** a popup asks on your first visit (Game > Travel > Galar). Until you choose, it's Sword; your choice is kept.
- **Dynamax:** leaders' aces Dynamax, or Gigantamax where they do in the games. Your first Galar gym win gives the **Dynamax Band**. Then, once per battle in Galar (gyms, rematches, expeditions and roaming legendaries met there), click the purple **◆ Dynamax** button, then a move: for three turns your Pokémon has more HP and uses Max Moves, which also set weather, terrain or stat changes. Hover a Max Move to see what it does.
- **Kubfu** joins you after your first win against Milo, at Lv 10. After that, more Kubfu can turn up in Galar's wild, and they're more often shiny.
- **Kubfu's towers** (Expeditions, 4 Galar badges, a Kubfu in your team or PC): clear the Tower of Darkness or the Tower of Waters, then pick one of your Kubfu. It evolves into Urshifu: Single Strike Style (Fighting/Dark, learns Wicked Blow) or Rapid Strike Style (Fighting/Water, learns Surging Strikes). Climb again for the next one.

## Paldea
- **Where to next?** Paldea's gyms and Titans can be done in any order. When you arrive, and after each one, a popup asks where you're heading (Game > Expeditions > Where to next... reopens it). Levels follow the order: your first gym is the easiest, whichever leader it is. After 8 badges: the Elite Four, Geeta and Nemona.
- **Titans** (optional, no badge): Klawf, Bombirdier, Orthworm, Great Tusk and Dondozo. A Titan starts with an aura. Knock it down once and it eats a Herba Mystica: back to full HP and stronger for round two. A win gives **Herba Mystica** (eat it from the bag: shiny Pokémon are more likely for a while) and the chance to catch the Titan's species.
- **Terastallization:** Paldea leaders' aces Terastallize into a type that changes from fight to fight, often one that counters your team, and their weakest move becomes Tera Blast. Your first Paldea gym win gives the **Tera Orb**. Your Pokémon needs to hold a **Tera Shard** of a type: click the cyan **◈ Tera** button, then a move. That move becomes **Tera Blast** of the shard's type for the rest of the battle and is used at once, and your Pokémon takes on the shard's type. Once per battle, in Paldea's fights. Hover "Tera ready" in the status line for the details.
- **Tera Shards:** beating a Paldea leader drops the shard of the type their ace used against you, so rematches let you collect more types.
- **The Treasures of Ruin:** four shrines, each sealed by four stakes of one colour (purple, yellow, green, blue). The stakes turn up now and then while you review in Paldea; finding all 16 takes about as long as the region itself, a little longer. You never get more than four of a colour. Then that shrine's expedition opens (see the table).
- **Area Zero:** clear an Area Zero expedition (Koraidon or Miraidon) and Area Zero appears in Game > Travel; it isn't listed before. It's inside Paldea (Paldea's gyms, badges and level cap), and its wild Pokémon are the Paradox Pokémon, who live nowhere else.

## Totem trials (Alola)
- **Gumshoos, Araquanid, Alolan Marowak, Lurantis, Togedemaru, Mimikyu, Ribombee and Kommo-o**, at their Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon levels (Ribombee Lv 46 so the ladder keeps climbing).
- **The battle:**
  - the totem starts alone with its aura (its real USUM boost, e.g. Mimikyu +1 to every stat);
  - it can't be knocked out early: while it still has help to call, no hit takes it below 30% HP (it holds on);
  - at 30% HP it calls for help and withdraws;
  - three reinforcements come one after another (its real allies, then Alola Pokémon of its type);
  - after the last one, the totem comes back at half HP with its aura;
  - knocking out the totem ends the trial.
- **After a win:** a guaranteed catch of the totem's species (a normal Pokémon, no aura) and the island's Z-Crystal, plus cash and EXP like a gym. Kahunas give Z-Crystals too (see Z-Moves below).
- **Pacing:** a totem opens after 2/3 of your gym interval; a kahuna right after its island's last totem.
- Totems are one-time; kahunas, the Elite Four and Hau can be rematched.
- If you'd already beaten Hala, Olivia or Nanu, those wins carry over.

## Z-Moves (Alola)
- Your **first totem trial** gives the **Z-Ring**. Owning it is all it takes: nothing to equip.
- **Give a Z-Crystal to hold** (in the bag or the PC). Only Pokémon that know a move of the crystal's type are offered. In gym and expedition battles that Pokémon can turn one move of that type into its Z-Move (Thunderbolt with Electrium Z becomes Gigavolt Havoc), **once per battle**. Status moves become Z-status moves with their bonus effect, as in the games.
- **In the battle window:** a gold **✦ Z-Move ready** button appears when your Pokémon can use one, the moves that can become Z-Moves are marked ✦ (hover for the Z-Move's type and power, or a status Z-Move's bonus; with Z-Move on, each button shows its Z-Move and power), and the status line shows "✦ Z ready" or "(Z used)". Click the button, then the move. Auto-battle spends it on the leader's last Pokémon, or when yours is low or about to be knocked out.
- **Held items now work in gym, expedition and legendary battles** (Z-Crystals included); before, they were left behind. The gym team list shows what each Pokémon holds.
- Leaders never use Z-Moves. Z-Moves, like Dynamax and Terastallization, need the showdown package; the built-in engine and wild battles don't have them.

## Themed loot
- A gym win drops 2 items from **the leader's own pool** instead of 2 random items:
  - every TM of their type - attacks and support moves (setup, status, screens, hazards, weather), two-turn moves like Dive included - unlocking in three bands as the rematch versions get stronger;
  - TMs of standout moves their rematch teams use;
  - the type's power item (Hard Stone, Charcoal…) and its evolution stone, where there is one;
  - healing items that get better with the version (Hyper Potion and Full Heal first, up to Full Restore and Max Revive).
- TMs you already own aren't dropped again.
- Paldea's leaders also drop the Tera Shard their ace used (see Paldea).

## Gym Rematches
- **Game > Expeditions > Gym Rematches:** fight a leader you've already beaten in the region you're in. Pick one to see their team and the chance of each drop.
- **They come back stronger:** 5 versions per leader, from the original fight up (Elite Four and Champions included), with evolved Pokémon and new moves at the right levels. The level follows your three strongest Pokémon +3 (within your level cap), never below the original fight.
- **Always 3 vs 3:** leaders whose own team has two Pokémon (Brock, Misty, Falkner, Whitney, Chuck, Roxanne, Brawly, Tate and Liza, Cilan, Lenora, Viola, Grant, Milo) bring a third of their type from their own generation in rematches, e.g. Brock's Aerodactyl and Misty's Lapras.
- **Rematch movesets hit back:** each Pokémon has its best attack, its best attack of another type and two support moves, so a team built on one counter type doesn't walk through. No Explosion or Self-Destruct.
- **One rematch or expedition per gym interval** (the same number of cards as between gyms): when it's ready, choose either. Cards in every region count, and it doesn't stack. Starting one uses it, win or lose.
- **Rewards:** cash and EXP scale with the level, plus 2-4 drops from the leader's themed loot (more and better at higher versions).
- Rematches never change your badges, gym progress or gym timers.
- A small "rematch ready" notice. Turn it off in Settings > Battle > Wild Battle Popups > Show Rematch Ready Popup.
- Needs the **wild** package too (the Expeditions menu comes with it).

## Fixes
- **Without the showdown package**, gyms with two of one species (Morty, Flannery, Elesa; now also Jasmine, Clair, Norman and Lance) never started. They work now.
- The built-in engine's battle log said stats "rose drastically" when they fell (Tail Whip, Scary Face). Only the text was wrong; it's fixed.

## Works with
- **showdown:** battles follow the real games' rules. Without it, a built-in engine is used.
- **wild:** travel to the other regions, with their gyms, level caps in wild battles, and the Expeditions menu. Without it, gyms are Kanto only.
