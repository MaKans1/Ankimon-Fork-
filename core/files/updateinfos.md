## Ankimon 2.0

Welcome to Ankimon 2.0, a fan-made overhaul of Ankimon:

- **Gyms in all nine regions:** Kanto's full Red/Blue ladder plus the first gyms of every other region, with badges and level caps.
- **Travel** between regions, each with its own Pokémon, gyms and progress.
- **Smarter wild battles,** wild levels that follow your badges, and team rotation.
- **Legendary expeditions**, from Zapdos to Wo-Chien.
- **Real game battle rules** for gyms (Pokémon Showdown).
- **Pacing** tuned to your daily card goal (Settings > Study).
